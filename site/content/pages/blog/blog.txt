A **stream**: this page says `AllowedChildTemplates: article`, so only
`article` pages can be added below it, and `blog.twig` lists the live ones
with `child_pages()`. Dates come from `PublishedAt`, the text under each title
from the article's `perex` page field. A third article is scheduled for 2030
and is not listed yet. More in [Streams](/guide/streams).

A **stream**: this page says `AllowedChildTemplates: article, blog-categories`,
so only articles and the [categories page](/blog/categories) can be added
below it, and `blog.twig` lists the articles with `child_pages()`. Dates come
from `PublishedAt`, the text under each title from the article's `perex` page
field, the coloured badges from its `categories` field. A third article is
scheduled for 2030 and is not listed yet. More in [Streams](/guide/streams).

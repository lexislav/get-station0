In Station0 every page is just a plain `.txt` file. There is no database to
migrate and no build step — you edit text, save, and the page is live.

## Front matter

The block above the `---` separator is **front matter**. It sets the title,
publish state, template, and any custom fields:

```
Title: My Article
Published: true
Template: article
PublishedAt: 2026-05-10 09:00
---
```

Everything below the separator is the body, written in Markdown.

## Why this is nice

- Content is **Git-friendly** — diffs are readable, history is free.
- Moving a page renames its folder; assets travel with it.
- No admin lock-in: edit in the browser *or* in your editor.

- type: text
  body: |-
    Every article is a folder under `site/content/pages/blog/` with an
    `article.txt` file. Its front matter:

    ```
    Title: How this blog works
    Status: published
    PublishedAt: 2026-10-20 09:00
    Author: Clara Weiss
    Perex: Articles are pages with the article template — …
    ---
    ```

    - `PublishedAt` is the date shown to readers.
    - `Author` is shown next to it.
    - `Perex` is a page field declared in `article.blocks.yaml`; the blog
      list shows it under the title.
    - `Categories` is a list of selects filled from the `blog-categories`
      collection — the coloured badges. Click one to see the
      [articles of that category](/blog/categories).
- type: quote
  text: Save an article in the admin and the "Log blog saves" task runs by itself.
  author: The task hook in site/tasks/log-blog-saves.php
- type: callout
  tone: tip
  title: Scheduling
  body: |-
    Set `PublishAt` to a future date and the article goes live by itself —
    like [the one coming in 2030](/blog/coming-soon), which you can open only
    because you are signed in. See [Visibility](/guide/visibility).

Pages in Station0 are hierarchical — `/about/team` lives under `/about`. But a
blog or news section is a **content stream**: a flat list of same-shaped items
under one parent.

## Template-based child constraints

A parent page can declare which child templates it accepts:

```
Title: Blog
Template: blog
AllowedChildTemplates: article
---
```

With this set, the admin only lets you create `article` pages under `/blog`.
Creating a plain `page` child — or dragging an unrelated page into the stream —
is rejected with a clear error. Leave the field empty and there is no
restriction (the default for every existing page).

## Why constrain?

- Keeps a stream **consistent** — every entry renders the same way.
- Makes the structure **self-documenting** for editors.
- Prevents accidental mixing of unrelated content.

Title: Admin menu groups
Status: published
Sort: 80
Summary: Give a part of the site its own tab in the admin's sidebar — pages and collections together, optionally only for some roles. Not the site's menu.
---
- type: text
  body: |
    An **admin menu group** is a tab in the **admin's sidebar** — it has
    nothing to do with the menu visitors see on the site (that one is built
    by `top_level_pages()` in `layout.twig`, see [Templates](/guide/templates)).

    Out of the box the sidebar has tabs for the page *Structure*, *Streams*
    and *Collections*. A group adds a tab of its own, so an editor finds
    everything about one part of the site in one place. It collects:

    - a page and its whole subtree — `Group: Shop` in the page's front matter;
    - collections — `group: Shop` in `_collection.yaml`.

    On this site:

    | Tab | Holds | Visible to |
    |---|---|---|
    | 🛒 **Shop** | the `/shop` subtree + the `categories` collection | editors and admins |
    | 📣 **Marketing** | the `/studio` page + the `banners` and `shared-blocks` collections | admins |

    The `people` collection has no group, so it stays in the generic
    *Collections* tab.

    Labels, icons, role limits and the tab order live in
    `site/content/_groups.yaml`:

    ```yaml
    shop:
      label: Shop
      icon: "🛒"
      roles: [editor]   # admins always pass
    marketing:
      label: Marketing
      icon: "📣"
      roles: [admin]
    ```

    For collections the roles are enforced; for pages they only hide the
    tab — the pages stay editable in *Structure*.

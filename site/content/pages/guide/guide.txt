Station0 in fifteen short chapters. Each one explains a feature and points
to the page of this site that uses it — open that page in the
[admin](/admin) next to it to see how it is made.

This page is a small **stream**: its front matter says
`AllowedChildTemplates: topic`, so the admin only lets you add `topic` pages
below it, and `guide.twig` lists them with `child_pages()`. The line under
each title is the topic's `summary` page field, read with `page_fields()`.

Title: Streams
Status: published
Sort: 50
Summary: A blog or a news section: a parent page that accepts only one kind of child page and lists them.
Demo: /blog
---
- type: text
  body: |
    A **stream** is a page with `AllowedChildTemplates` in its front matter:

    ```
    Title: Blog
    AllowedChildTemplates: article
    ---
    ```

    - In the admin, new pages under `/blog` can only use the `article`
      template, and the *Streams* tab lists the records of every stream with
      their state, date and author.
    - The template lists the children:

    ```twig
    {% for child in child_pages(page.urlPath) %}
      <a href="{{ child.urlPath }}">{{ child.title }}</a>
      {{ child.publishedAt|date('j M Y') }}
      {{ page_fields(child).perex }}
    {% endfor %}
    ```

    `child_pages()` returns only live, listed children, sorted by `Sort`.
    Articles carry `PublishedAt` (the date shown) and `Author`.

    A comma list allows several templates: `AllowedChildTemplates: article, video`.

    This site has three streams: the [Blog](/blog) (articles), the
    [Shop](/shop) (products) and this [Guide](/guide) (topics).

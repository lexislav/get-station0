A demo catalogue for [page fields](/guide/page-fields). Each product is a
page with the `product` template; price, stock, colour, hero image, category,
specifications, datasheet and related products are **typed page fields**
declared in `site/templates/product.blocks.yaml`. The cards below read them
with `page_fields(child)`.

The page has `Group: Shop`, so in the admin it sits — with its products and
the `categories` collection — in its own **🛒 Shop** tab.

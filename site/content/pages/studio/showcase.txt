This page is built from **collections** — content with no URL of its own,
kept in `site/content/collections/` and pulled in by the `showcase`
template. Edit the people in the admin's *Collections → People*; the page
follows. Nobody typed the people's names as titles: the `people` collection
builds each title from the *First name* and *Surname* fields
(`title: "{name} {surname}"` in its `_collection.yaml`). More in
[Collections](/guide/collections).

The page has `Group: Marketing`, so in the admin it shares the
**📣 Marketing** tab with the `banners` and `shared-blocks` collections.
